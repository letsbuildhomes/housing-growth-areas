About this data

Eligibility reflects the publisher’s likely interim Act 250 exemption eligibility, not Tier 1B approval. Each row represents one published area. Unknown means the criterion is missing or not reported; it does not mean “No.”

Infrastructure: at least one. Drinking water or sewer / wastewater or adequate soils. Any Yes means Met; all No means Not met; otherwise Unknown.

Downtowns and village centers need both requirements. “Not separately required” means zoning is not stated as a separate condition in the exemption clause; it does not mean there are no zoning obligations. The source zoning value remains unreported. These display groupings do not recalculate publisher eligibility or evaluate every project-specific requirement.

Municipality groups use the source’s labels, which can include locality names. Missing municipality labels would appear under “Unknown municipality.”

These are the first three layers of the Vermont interim Act 250 map. The page uses a saved snapshot, not a live feed.

In the final step, town shading indicates at least one likely eligible area (teal), designated areas but none likely eligible (coral), or no designated areas (taupe). Counts use recorded municipality membership with a reviewed name crosswalk, not buffer overlaps or whole-town exemptions. Shared areas count for both named municipalities but remain one area. Area polygons reflect the publisher’s status: teal for likely eligible, coral for likely ineligible, gray for unknown.
